ABOUT MUSIC & MIND

Curiosity at the intersection of music and the mind.

Music & Mind is an independent initiative by Raja Sharath Chandra, a Trinity Certified music educator interested in music, psychology and human wellbeing.

The work currently spans guitar, keyboard, flute, music theory and musical analysis, while leaving space for the platform to develop naturally.
